## How policies work

- **Review dates.** Yearly by default. A review with no change moves the date
  on and nobody re-accepts. A review that changes the policy publishes a new
  version, and staff accept it afresh. Reviews show in Who's due.
- **Who must read what.** Each policy is set for carers, office staff,
  managers or everyone, with a deadline to accept. Reminders go out; late
  acceptances show in Who's due and the weekly email. New starters get their
  set when they're hired (RECRUITMENT.md).
- **A company's own policies.** Uploaded as Word or PDF, with acceptances
  tracked the same way.
- **Updates from us.** When the law or CQC guidance changes and we update a
  template, companies that adopted it are told what changed and asked to
  review their copy (organisation_policies already records the template
  version it came from).
- **Understanding checks.** A few short questions after reading, switched on
  per policy by the company — for the policies inspectors probe (safeguarding,
  whistleblowing, medication). AI can draft the questions; a manager checks
  them.
- **Linked to the features that use them.** The complaints guidance points to
  the company's Complaints policy; disciplinary letters name its Disciplinary
  policy; the safeguarding process links to its Safeguarding policy.
- **AI fills in templates** from the company's own details (name, address,
  registered manager, service area), for a manager to check. 1.5 times cost.
- **Who publishes.** Office staff can prepare a policy; only a manager
  publishes it, because it's the company's official policy.
- **Records.** Acceptances go on the carer's file and into their evidence pack
  (not done yet), and a compliance view shows each policy and each person.

## 1. Purpose

Good care depends on knowing, day and night, whether residents are safe and well looked after, and acting quickly when they are not. This policy sets out how {{organisation}} checks the quality and safety of the home, who is responsible, and how we keep improving.

## 2. Scope

It applies to the directors, the nominated individual, the registered manager and deputy, shift leaders, maintenance and ancillary staff, and all care staff. It covers care, staffing on every shift, the building and its systems, records and the business.

## 3. The law and guidance

- Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Regulated Activities) Regulations 2014, Regulation 17: Good governance — systems to assess, monitor and improve quality and safety; to assess, monitor and reduce risks; to keep accurate records for each resident, for staff and for the running of the home; and to seek and act on feedback. CQC can ask for a written report on this within 28 days.
- Regulation 15: Premises and equipment — the home is clean, secure, suitable and properly maintained.
- Care Quality Commission (Registration) Regulations 2009, Regulation 12: Statement of purpose — a written statement of our aims, services and who we provide them for, kept under review, with any revision sent to CQC within 28 days.
- Regulation 20A of the 2014 Regulations: Requirement as to display of performance assessments — our CQC rating displayed in the home and on our website, within 21 days of its publication.
- Regulations 5, 6, 7 and 19 of the 2014 Regulations — directors, the nominated individual, the registered manager and staff are fit and proper (see Fit and Proper Persons).
- CQC's Single Assessment Framework, the well-led key question — its quality statements look for a shared direction and open culture, capable leaders, staff free to speak up, sound governance, management and sustainability, partnership working, and continual learning and improvement.
- Data Security and Protection Toolkit — completed every year (see Data Security and Breaches).

## 4. Our policy

- Quality is checked in everyday work, on every shift, not once a year before an inspection.
- Every check has a frequency, an owner and a record. What it finds is acted on, and the action is checked.
- We ask residents, families and staff what they think, and show them what we changed.

## 5. How we are organised

{{nominated_individual}} supervises the management of the regulated activity for the company, and reports to the directors. {{registered_manager}} manages the home and is accountable to the nominated individual. Every shift has a named shift leader, and a manager is always on call.

## 6. How we check quality

### 6.1 The building and the environment

- Building — the registered manager walks round the whole home every month, as Health and Safety sets out, including doors and window restrictors, and checks the maintenance schedule is in date.
- Fire — the fire risk assessment is reviewed every year and whenever anything significant changes; the fire safety log, drills and personal emergency evacuation plans are checked every month against Fire Safety.
- Water — the checks in the legionella scheme of control, including flushing and temperatures, are checked every month against Legionella and Water Safety.
- Infection control — the whole home, including laundry and sluices, audited at least every three months.
- Kitchen — food safety records checked every month, and the local authority's food hygiene inspection acted on.

### 6.2 Care and records

- Medicines — audited every month, as Medicines Management sets out.
- Care plans — checked every month by the key worker, with a sample audited by the registered manager.
- Falls, pressure areas, weight and nutrition — reviewed every month for each resident and across the home.
- Deprivation of liberty — every application, authorisation, condition and expiry date tracked every month (see Deprivation of Liberty).
- Residents' money — audited every month, as Residents' Money and Property sets out.

### 6.3 Staffing, day and night

- Dependency is reviewed every month and sets the staffing on each shift, as Safe Staffing, Rotas and Agency Staff sets out. Night staffing is checked against residents' needs at night and the number of staff the fire evacuation plan needs.
- Call bell response times, and falls and incidents by time of day, are reviewed every month.
- The registered manager or deputy makes an unannounced visit at night or at a weekend at least every three months, to see care, staffing and the building as they really are, and records what they find.

### 6.4 Staff

Each person's file on the site shows who is due or overdue for training on the training matrix, supervision every three months, appraisal every year, spot checks every 12 weeks, competency assessments every year, and policies to accept. A sample of staff files is checked every three months for complete recruitment records.

### 6.5 Incidents, complaints and safeguarding

Incidents, complaints, safeguarding concerns and duty of candour cases go through the site's concerns process. The registered manager reviews them together every month for patterns by shift, unit or time of day, and checks every notification was made.

### 6.6 Feedback

- Residents' meetings — held at least every three months, in a way residents can take part in. What was raised and what we did is recorded and shared, and residents who do not come are asked individually.
- Families — asked at reviews and relatives' meetings, and sent a written survey at least once a year.
- Staff — asked at supervision, handovers and team meetings, and through a yearly staff survey. They can raise concerns at any time, including under Whistleblowing.
- Professionals and commissioners — their feedback and contract monitoring are acted on.

## 7. The monthly report and the governance meeting

Each month {{registered_manager}} sends {{nominated_individual}} a short quality report: dependency, staffing and agency use day and night, overdue training and supervision, incidents and falls, complaints, safeguarding, notifications, audit results, building issues, and the improvement plan.

The registered manager and the nominated individual hold a governance meeting at least every three months, and the directors attend or receive its notes. It reviews the reports, the risk register, the improvement plan, policies due for review, the statement of purpose, the Data Security and Protection Toolkit, and anything CQC or commissioners have raised. Actions are minuted, each with a named person and a date.

The nominated individual also visits the home at least every three months, walks round it, and talks to residents, families and staff on duty.

## 8. The risk register and the improvement plan

The business risk register, including risks from the building such as the roof, heating or lift, is reviewed at the governance meeting, and so at least every three months, as Risk Management sets out, and sooner whenever a new risk arises.

Everything our checks, feedback, inspections and contract monitoring show needs to change goes onto one improvement plan, with a named person and date for each action. The registered manager closes an action only once it is done and has worked.

## 9. Our duties to CQC

- The statement of purpose is reviewed every year and whenever the service changes, and CQC is sent any revision within 28 days.
- Our current CQC rating is displayed in the home, where people coming in will see it, and on our website, within 21 days of its publication.
- Notifications, including of the registered manager's absence, are made as Reportable Incidents and Registered Manager Absence and Deputising set out.
- Directors, the nominated individual and the registered manager make a yearly fit and proper persons declaration.

## 10. Roles and responsibilities

- **Directors** — are accountable for the home's quality and safety, and provide the resources it needs, including for the building.
- **Nominated individual** — supervises the management of the home, holds the registered manager to account, chairs the governance meeting, visits the home, and makes sure CQC is told what it must be.
- **Registered manager** — runs the checks, writes the monthly report, keeps the improvement plan, and acts on what is found.
- **Shift leaders** — check the home and staffing at the start of each shift, and report what they find.
- **Maintenance staff** — carry out and record the building, fire and water checks.
- **Care staff** — keep accurate records, and report incidents, faults and concerns.

## 11. Training

The registered manager and nominated individual keep up to date with CQC's framework and the regulations. Staff who carry out audits, spot checks and building checks are trained to do so.

## 12. Records

Audits, safety logs, reports, minutes, the risk register, the improvement plan, residents' meeting notes and surveys are kept by the registered manager for the periods set in Record Keeping and Retention, and shown to CQC and commissioners on request.

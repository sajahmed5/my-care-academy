## 1. Purpose

The people who run a care service set its standards. This policy sets out how {{organisation}} makes sure that its directors, its nominated individual, its registered manager and everyone it employs are fit and proper to do so, how we check this before they start and keep checking while they are with us, and what we do if a concern arises.

## 2. Scope

It applies to:

- directors and anyone in an equivalent role, such as a partner or the owner;
- the nominated individual, who supervises the management of the regulated activity on behalf of the company;
- the registered manager;
- everyone else we employ or engage to carry out the regulated activity, including bank and agency staff.

## 3. The law and guidance

- Health and Social Care Act 2008 (Regulated Activities) Regulations 2014, Regulation 5: Fit and proper persons: directors — directors and equivalents must be of good character, have the qualifications, competence, skills and experience their role needs, be able to do it by reason of their health after reasonable adjustments, not have been responsible for serious misconduct or mismanagement in providing a regulated activity, and not be in a category the regulation excludes.
- Regulation 6 of the same Regulations — the nominated individual must be of good character, and have the qualifications, competence, skills and experience the role needs.
- Regulation 7: Requirements relating to registered managers — the registered manager must be fit, with the information in Schedule 3 available about them.
- Regulation 19: Fit and proper persons employed — everyone employed is of good character, competent and able to do their work, with the information in Schedule 3 available.
- Schedule 3 — the information we must hold, including proof of identity, a criminal record check, evidence of conduct in previous care work, why previous care work ended, qualifications, a full employment history with gaps explained, and evidence of health.
- Schedule 4, Part 1 — the people who cannot be directors of a provider, including undischarged bankrupts and others subject to insolvency restrictions, people on a barred list, and anyone prohibited by law from holding the office, such as a disqualified company director.
- Care Quality Commission guidance for providers on Regulations 5, 6, 7 and 19.

## 4. Our policy

- Nobody takes up a role covered by this policy until the checks below are complete and satisfactory.
- We keep checking while people are in their role, not only when they start.
- Where a concern arises, we act promptly and proportionately, and tell CQC where we must.
- We keep records that show how each person was found fit.

## 5. Directors, the nominated individual and the registered manager

### 5.1 Before appointment

Before someone becomes a director, nominated individual or registered manager, we:

- check their identity, with a recent photograph;
- obtain an enhanced DBS check, with a check of the barred lists where the role is eligible;
- check their full employment history and explanations of any gaps;
- obtain references, including from any previous health or social care employer, and find out why that work ended;
- check their qualifications and, for the registered manager, that they have the experience and training to manage the service;
- check the public registers that apply: the insolvency register, the register of disqualified company directors, and any professional register they are on;
- search for any public information that suggests serious misconduct or mismanagement, such as findings by a regulator, tribunal or court;
- ask them to sign a declaration that they meet the requirements and are not in an excluded category;
- once an offer has been made, ask about their health, so that we can make any reasonable adjustments they need.

The registered manager also applies to CQC to be registered, and CQC carries out its own assessment.

### 5.2 Every year

Each director, the nominated individual and the registered manager signs a fresh declaration every year, and the register checks are repeated. They must tell us straight away if anything changes — a criminal charge or conviction, bankruptcy, a finding by a regulator, or a health matter that affects their role.

> Say how often you renew enhanced DBS checks for directors, the nominated individual, the registered manager and staff, or that you check them through the DBS update service.

## 6. Everyone we employ

Everyone we employ is checked before they start, as our Recruitment policy and Right to Work and Sponsored Workers policy set out, and their fitness is kept under review through induction, supervision, spot checks, competency assessments, training and the renewal of checks. They must tell us straight away if they are arrested, charged, cautioned or convicted, or if anything happens that affects their fitness to work.

Agency staff are checked by their agency, and we obtain written confirmation of those checks before they work.

## 7. When a concern arises

If information comes to light that suggests someone may no longer be fit and proper:

- the person responsible — the nominated individual, or for a concern about the nominated individual, another director — looks into it promptly and fairly, giving the person the chance to respond;
- while it is looked into, they consider whether the person should step back from some or all of their duties, to protect the people we support;
- the outcome is decided and recorded, with the reasons.

For a director, the nominated individual or the registered manager who is found unfit, we remove them from the role, and tell CQC as the regulations and our registration require. For an employee, we follow our Disciplinary policy, and refer them to the Disclosure and Barring Service and to any professional regulator where the law requires.

Concerns about fitness can be raised by anyone, including through our Whistleblowing policy, and will be taken seriously.

## 8. Roles and responsibilities

- **Directors** — make sure this policy is followed for each other and for the nominated individual, and declare anything that affects their own fitness.
- **Nominated individual** — makes sure checks are done for the registered manager and that the company keeps the records Schedule 3 requires, and acts on concerns.
- **Registered manager** — makes sure checks are done for everyone else employed, and acts on concerns.

## 9. Records

For each director, the nominated individual and the registered manager we keep a file showing the checks done, declarations, and any concerns and how they were resolved. Staff records are kept on their file on the site. Records are kept while the person is in their role and for six years after.

## 1. Purpose

Rest matters, for your health and for the care you give. This policy explains how much leave you are entitled to, how to book it, and how we decide requests so that everyone gets their leave and the people we support still get their care.

## 2. Scope

It applies to all employees of {{organisation}}, including those on zero-hours and irregular-hours contracts. Sickness, family leave and other kinds of time off are covered by their own policies.

## 3. The law and guidance

- Working Time Regulations 1998 — the statutory minimum of 5.6 weeks' paid leave a year, how holiday pay is calculated, and, for irregular-hours and part-year workers, how leave is worked out.
- Employment Rights Act 1996 — the right not to suffer a detriment for taking leave you are entitled to.
- Your contract of employment — your own entitlement, which may be more than the minimum.

## 4. Your entitlement

Full-time employees are entitled to at least 5.6 weeks' paid leave a year, which may include bank holidays. Part-time employees receive the same entitlement in proportion to the hours they work. Employees on irregular hours accrue leave in line with the Working Time Regulations as they apply at the time. Your contract and your record in the HR pack show your allowance, in days or hours.

## 5. Booking leave

### 5.1 Requests

Request leave from your own login on the site, or ask the office to enter it for you. Give as much notice as you can: at least twice as many days' notice as the length of leave you are asking for, and longer for the busy periods below. Do not book travel until your leave is approved.

### 5.2 Deciding requests

Requests are approved or declined in writing, with a reason if declined, normally within five working days. Managers and office staff approve care staff's leave; only a manager approves office staff's leave, so nobody approves their own. Before approving, we check who else is off: the people we support must still receive their care, and we may limit how many people can be off at once in a team or area. Requests are generally decided in the order they are made, with fairness over the year in mind.

### 5.3 Busy periods

Christmas, New Year and the school summer holidays are the hardest times to cover. Leave over these periods is shared fairly, and we may ask you to book it earlier than usual.

### 5.4 Cancelling leave

You may cancel approved leave with reasonable notice. In exceptional circumstances, such as an emergency affecting the safety of the people we support, we may need to ask you to change approved leave. We will give at least as much notice as the law requires, and will reimburse costs you cannot recover.

## 6. Taking your leave

You are encouraged to take all your leave within the leave year. Leave cannot normally be carried over, except where the law allows it — for example, if you could not take it because of sickness or family leave — or where your manager agrees in advance in exceptional circumstances.

> State any carry-over you allow by agreement, and the deadline for using it.

Leave cannot be replaced by payment except when your employment ends.

## 7. Holiday pay

Holiday pay is calculated as the Working Time Regulations require. For the first four weeks of your leave, it includes regular overtime, commission and other payments you normally receive for your work, averaged over the reference period the law sets.

## 8. When you leave

When your employment ends, you are paid for leave you have accrued but not taken. If you have taken more leave than you have accrued, the difference may be deducted from your final pay where your contract allows it. You may be asked to take outstanding leave during your notice period.

## 9. Sickness and leave

If you are ill during leave, see our Sickness Absence policy: you may be able to take those days another time.

## 10. Roles and responsibilities

- **Registered manager** — makes sure leave is managed fairly and that everyone can take their entitlement, and decides requests from office staff.
- **Office staff** — keep allowances and requests up to date, decide care staff requests, and arrange cover.
- **Employees** — book leave in good time, take their entitlement, and do not work during approved leave.

## 11. Records

Allowances, requests, decisions and leave taken are recorded in the HR pack, and kept for six years, as the law requires for holiday and pay records.

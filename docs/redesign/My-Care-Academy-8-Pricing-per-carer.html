Care training pricing — questions people ask

Is there a per-learner charge?
Only on Training only, where you pay for each carer on the system every month Assumption: per month. Growth, Pro and Enterprise are one flat price for your organisation with unlimited learners.
How is the per-carer price worked out?
In bands, like income tax: your first 15 carers are £5 each, carers 16 to 25 are £4.50, 26 to 50 are £4 and anyone over 50 is £3.50. A team of 20 pays 15 × £5 plus 5 × £4.50, which is £97.50 a month.
Who counts as a carer?
Anyone with a learner account that month. Managers and admin users are free on every plan. Assumption
What happens when someone leaves?
Mark them as a leaver and they stop counting from the next bill. Their certificates and training record stay on file.
Can I start with training and add a package later?
Yes. Move up whenever you like; the per-carer charge stops and the flat package price takes over.
What happens when my team passes 72 carers?
At 73 carers, Training only would cost more than Growth, which is £300 a month for unlimited carers and includes the HR pack. We'll ask whether you'd like to move to Growth. You can stay on Training only if you prefer.
Which courses are included?
The full library of 59 CQC-aligned care courses, with interactive content, a 20-question assessment and a branded, verifiable certificate for each.
Is there a contract?
Growth, Pro and Enterprise are a 12-month contract, billed monthly. Training only is monthly rolling. Prices exclude VAT. Assumption
Why is Enterprise "from" £750?
Enterprise includes face-to-face training, Care Certificate assessment and bespoke reports, which we deliver for your service rather than switch on. £750 a month covers a typical provider; we quote a firm figure once we know your size.

Decisions to confirm before building

  1. Where the leaderboard lives. Drawn here as a Stars tab in the Staff section row (People → Staff), next to Training matrix. The other option is a card on Today showing the top three with a link through.
  2. Where a person's history lives. Drawn as its own Stars tab on the file, placed after Training. It could instead sit inside Training, or under More ▾, if the row is getting long.
  3. Office access. Today only managers can read the in-content star rows at the database layer. Building this means updating that policy to the shared console-user check, so office staff see the same page as managers.
  4. Period. "This quarter" is drawn as a calendar quarter (Oct to Dec). The gain bars in the table are relative to the top earner for that period.
  5. What the history shows. One line per assessment attempt and one line per course per day for questions along the way, with +0 lines kept so the rules are visible. History is derived from existing records, so it goes back to each carer's first attempt with no backfill needed.
  6. Who is listed. Everyone with a learner account in the company, including leavers for the period they were here? Drawn as current staff only, with "No stars yet" counted separately so new starters are not shamed at the bottom.
  7. Carers themselves. This mock-up is console-only. Whether carers should see the leaderboard in Learn is a separate decision and is not drawn.
  8. Package. Stars already exist on every package, so the leaderboard would too unless you want it from Growth.

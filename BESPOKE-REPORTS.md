# Bespoke reports — agreed plan (29 Oct 2026)

Agreed with Saj on 29 Oct 2026. Nothing below is built yet. Bespoke reports
are Enterprise (see PACKAGES.md): the company tells us what it wants, we build
it, and only that company sees it.

## Today

`/org/reports` (Evidence & exports) links four standard training reports —
who's up to date, every assignment, certificates and renewals, time taken —
and each carer's evidence pack. Nothing is bespoke.

## Asking for a report

An Enterprise manager asks on the Reports page: what they want to know, who
it's for (CQC, the council, their directors), how often (one-off, weekly,
monthly) and the format (on screen, PDF, Excel). The request shows where it
is — asked, being built, ready — and the manager and we can message on it to
clarify.

**Unlimited requests**, each **ready within 10 working days**.

## How we build them

With a **report builder only we use**, in platform admin: choose the data
(training, certificates, supervisions, audits, concerns, HR, clients), the
filters, columns, grouping and any charts, and save the report to that one
company. Most reports need no new code, which is what makes the 10 days
realistic; anything unusual is built by hand. AI helps us build them; a
customer asking AI questions of their own data isn't part of this for now.

## What the company sees

- An **"Our reports"** section on its Reports page, seen only by that
  company — by managers and office staff, unless a manager keeps a report to
  managers only.
- Live data, downloadable as PDF or Excel, with the company's logo.
- **Scheduled sending** — a report emailed on a schedule to named people (a
  monthly PDF to the registered manager and the directors, say).

## Standard reports

Each add-on brings its own standard reports for everyone with it — HR
(absence, turnover, recruitment sources), Service audits (QA ratings,
governance report), Concerns (trends) — beside today's four training reports.
Bespoke is for anything beyond those.

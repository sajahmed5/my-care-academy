# Face-to-face training — agreed plan (29 Oct 2026)

Agreed with Saj on 29 Oct 2026. Nothing below is built yet beyond what's
marked as today's. Face-to-face training is Enterprise (see PACKAGES.md).

## Today

Both parts are the company's **own** training, not ours:

- **Classroom sessions** (`/org/classroom`, migration 20261026120000) — the
  company records a session its own trainer ran and who attended. A session
  covering a library course completes it for each attendee with a
  certificate, so it sits on the matrix like e-learning.
- **Care Certificate observations** (`/org/observations`) — the 16 standards
  observed in the workplace, by the company's assessors or ours; only the
  employer awards the certificate.

Training delivered by us isn't built, and the Manchester & Stockport page
says the training is all online.

## Our sessions

- **Where.** Our training office: Unit A1, The Embankment, Vale Road,
  Stockport, SK4 3GN.
- **What.** A face-to-face session for every course in the library (all 59),
  plus **induction training** and **annual refresher training**.
- **Included in the Enterprise price, with unlimited places.**
- **Online or face to face, or both** — a carer doesn't have to pass the online
  course before the session.
- **Booking.** A manager books named staff onto a session. Each person gets a
  confirmation email and a reminder the day before. Cancel up to 48 hours
  before; a no-show uses the place.
- **On the day.** Our trainer takes the register on a tablet and records each
  person's practical assessment, passed or not. Certificates are issued
  automatically and the matrix updates.
- **Outside Greater Manchester.** The booking page says plainly that sessions
  are in Stockport, with "ask us" for anything else.

## Our side (platform admin)

The booking system is built here — not an outside system (Saj, 29 Oct 2026).

- Set up sessions: the course, date, time, trainer and number of places.
- **Closed days.** Mark days when no training runs (bank holidays, the trainer
  away, the office shut) — nothing can be booked on them.
- See who's booked on each session, and take the register.

# Staff Audits — agreed plan (29 Oct 2026)

Agreed with Saj on 29 Oct 2026. Nothing below is built yet. Staff Audits is in
Growth and up (see PACKAGES.md). It was called "Staff records" until 29 Oct
2026; the name read like recruitment documents.

## The aim

Today a supervision is a date, a free-text summary and a next-due date, and a
spot check is an area, an outcome and a note. Nothing guides the manager
through either. The new one makes each audit a real form with real questions,
done on the site (on a phone in a client's home, for a spot check), signed in
the room, with what was agreed followed up.

Nothing has been recorded in today's version (no supervisions, competency
checks or staff documents on 29 Oct 2026), so nothing needs moving.

MyRoster's supervision, appraisal and spot-check forms (its Forms module, kinds
`carer_supervision`, `carer_appraisal`, `carer_spot_check`) are the starting
point, as with recruitment.

## The audits

| Audit | What it is | Due by default |
|---|---|---|
| **Supervision** | Questions on wellbeing, workload, the clients they visit, training needs and concerns. Last time's actions shown and reviewed; new actions agreed; both sign. | Every 3 months |
| **Appraisal** | The year looked back on, goals for the next, training plans. The carer fills in a short self-assessment on their own login beforehand. Both sign. | Every 12 months |
| **Spot check** | Unannounced, on a phone in the client's home. The client, picked from the client list (PACKAGES.md), then arrived on time, ID badge, uniform, PPE, hand hygiene, dignity, medication, records — each yes / no / not applicable with a comment — and an overall outcome. The carer signs. | Every 12 weeks |
| **Competency assessment** | One skill signed off against its own criteria. A separate form from the spot check. | Every 12 months, per skill |
| **Performance plan** | See below. | — |

**Competency areas**, each with its own criteria: medication, hoist, moving
and handling people, moving and handling objects, food and hydration support,
de-escalation, infection control, personal care and dignity. A company can add
its own areas and edit the criteria.

**Performance plans** belong to both Staff Audits and Complaints &
safeguarding: a plan is raised because of an issue, and the issue can come
from either. It lives in Staff Audits (Growth), raised from a supervision,
spot check or competency that wasn't met; with Complaints & safeguarding (Pro)
it can also be raised from a complaint or incident, and links back to it.
Today it's gated on Complaints & safeguarding only.

## How it works

- **Editable.** Every form, the competency areas and criteria, and each due
  period start from our defaults; a company can change them.
- **Booked dates.** A manager books a supervision or appraisal; the carer gets
  an email and a notification with the date and who with. Spot checks stay
  unannounced — no warning.
- **Who's due.** The existing lists and the weekly email show what's coming up
  and what's overdue, per audit and per competency area.
- **Signing.** In the room, on the manager's screen, as today. If the carer
  can't sign, the manager records why.
- **Follow-up.** Agreed actions go onto the carer's task list (built). A spot
  check or competency not met suggests training for the manager to confirm —
  never assigned automatically (built for incidents; extended to these).
- **What the carer sees.** Their own appraisal self-assessment to fill in, and
  their booked dates. Never the manager's notes, supervision records or spot
  checks (settled 23 Oct 2026, CARER-FILE.md §6).
- **Who they're for.** Carers, and office staff too for supervisions and
  appraisals (agreed 29 Oct 2026). Spot checks and competency are for carers
  only — they happen in clients' homes.
- **Who does them.** Managers and office workers. A senior carer holding a
  supervision needs an office login.
- **AI.** Optional, at 1.5 times cost like recruitment: Claude drafts the
  supervision summary and suggests actions from the notes; a manager checks
  it before it's saved.
- **Evidence pack.** Completed audits go into the carer's evidence pack PDF
  (built).
